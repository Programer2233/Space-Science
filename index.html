Our Mission

Who We Are

We are a community of curious minds united by our fascination with space. Founded in 2019, the Space Science Club brings together students passionate about astronomy, astrophysics, and space engineering.

From observing meteor showers to building satellite prototypes, we explore the universe through hands-on projects, research, and collaboration — making the infinite accessible to everyone.
